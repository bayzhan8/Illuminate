ILLUMINATE

Operations research, from nothing

The good ideas in this field are pictures.
They are almost never taught that way.

Optimisation is full of ideas that are genuinely simple once you can see them, and that are usually introduced as a page of algebra with a hence in the middle. This is an attempt to do it the other way round: build the picture first, move it around until it is obvious, and only then say what everyone else calls it.

Every number on every page is computed by code in this repository, in exact arithmetic, and re-checked by its tests. Nothing is rounded and nothing is taken on my word — make test and see.

01 · Linear programming duality

Every plan has a price tag

Every planning problem has a second problem hiding inside it, about prices, and solving either one solves both. Eleven chapters, six animations and four things to push around, built from a workshop with three shelves and two products. It ends with what a bottleneck is actually costing you, and why that number has an expiry date.
